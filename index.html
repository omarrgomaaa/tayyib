El Wadi El Jedid — The New Valley — is one of Egypt's most storied agricultural regions, stretching across ancient oases fed by subterranean waters from beneath the Sahara. For millennia, its soil has produced dates of extraordinary quality.

TAYYIB sources exclusively from family growers who tend their palms by hand, harvesting at peak ripeness under open sky — no additives, no shortcuts, nothing but the honest gift of the earth.
